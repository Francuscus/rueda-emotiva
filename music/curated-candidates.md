## Production checklist

1. Find a specifically licensed recording for each candidate (the composition's age does not determine recording rights).
2. Audition and identify a musically coherent excerpt of 40–60 seconds; avoid arbitrary cuts.
3. Document recording performer, recording source URL, license, attribution, start/end timestamps and emotional rationale.
4. Export MP3 only when the source license permits this use; retain license/attribution metadata.
5. Import via Pasajes musicales → Añadir pasajes, then Exportar colección; test playback and recording before deployment.
6. Replace any candidate that fails musical or licensing review. Do not use commercial YouTube audio without permission.

## Editorial caution

These are subjective emotion interpretations and a deliberately broad first pass, not claims that particular musical cultures or compositions intrinsically encode universal emotions. Some works are repeated across intensity levels as *candidate source compositions*; final production should choose distinct moments or alternative pieces.
